\section{Materials and Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

We remap four sources into one class list and split them, plant frames by recording (Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}). Four training sets, each changing one factor, are trained with one recipe and scored by the same evaluation code.


\textbf{Plant data.} We recorded the plant data on 16 July 2026 in the phosphoric-acid treatment unit of the Maroc Chimie complex (OCP Group, Safi), with a smartphone mounted at fixed positions in several locations and at several viewing angles, under varying light. The phone stands in for fixed surveillance cameras. There are 14 recordings: 12 videos taken between 16:35 and 16:48, and two short clips. We sampled frames across each recording and removed near-duplicates, which gave 207 frames at $1920\times1080$, annotated in Roboflow with seven classes (Table~\ref{tab:data}). The people in view are the authors, wearing site PPE. Plant frames have no absence (negative) labels, so the plant evaluation measures PPE detection and cannot measure violation detection. We use only original frames, without Roboflow augmentations, so no altered copy of a training frame can reach the test set. The 14 recordings form five folds of 40 to 43 frames, built by assigning the largest remaining recording to the lightest fold. Gloves and goggles occur in few recordings, so some folds contain none.

\textbf{Public data and class map.} Roboflow PPE-Detection \cite{roboflowppe} (308 construction images), SH17 \cite{ahmad2024sh17} (about 8{,}000 industrial images) and Roboflow gas masks \cite{roboflowgasmasks} (361 images) are remapped by class name into nine classes: Person, helmet, vest, gloves, boots, goggles, no\_helmet, no\_gloves and gas mask. no\_helmet and no\_gloves come only from Roboflow PPE-Detection, and SH17's body-part classes are dropped. Remapping by name rather than by numeric ID is necessary, since a naive merge had discarded about 43\% of training images as corrupt. Public sources are split 70/15/15 with a fixed seed, by original picture, so the 6 construction and 10 gas-mask pictures that were uploaded twice stay on one side. SH17 is capped at 1{,}500/300/300 images (uniform random, fixed seed), and 600 further SH17 training images are reserved for model S. In every run, no validation or test image is byte-identical to a training image, and a run stops if a plant recording appears on both sides.

\textbf{Four training sets.} Table~\ref{tab:models} lists the models, each differing from the previous one in one factor. B is the construction-only benchmark. We train it ourselves with the same recipe as the other models, so its architecture, checkpoint and settings are known. Going from B to P adds public data and classes, and going from P to S adds training-set size alone. From S to A, 497 further public images are replaced by about 165 plant frames shown three times, which keeps the number of training images per epoch the same and isolates the effect of plant imagery. S sees more distinct images than A, which makes it a conservative control.

\textbf{Training recipe.} We use Ultralytics 8.4.95 \cite{ultralytics2023} and YOLOv8n from COCO weights (3.0M parameters, 8.1 GFLOPs at 640), with input 640, batch 16, 50 epochs, AdamW (learning rate $7.7\times10^{-4}$, $\beta_1$ 0.9, weight decay $5\times10^{-4}$, 3 warm-up epochs; Ultralytics' automatic choice for runs under 10{,}000 iterations), HSV jitter, flips, random erasing (0.4) and mosaic (off for the last 10 epochs), on a Tesla T4 (Google Colab). The augmentations were chosen for plant conditions. Colour jitter imitates dust haze and glare, erasing imitates pipes and equipment hiding part of a worker, and mosaic covers the wide range of worker sizes across camera positions. All runs share one validation split of 398 public images. We validate only after the last epoch and keep the final weights, so no checkpoint is selected on any data. B, P and S use seeds 0, 1 and 2; A uses seed 0 in each fold.

\textbf{Test sets.} The fold-$k$ model of A is tested only on the recordings of fold $k$. B, P and S never see plant frames and are tested on all 207, so pooling A's five out-of-fold predictions puts every model on the same frames. The public test set has 401 images (47 construction, 300 SH17, 54 gas mask). Its SH17 and gas-mask images (354, called held-out images below) come from the same sources as training images, so they do not test transfer to a new setting.

\textbf{Scoring and uncertainty.} One evaluation script computes every number (Table~\ref{tab:protocol}). Each model predicts once per test set at a confidence floor of 0.001 (NMS IoU 0.7, at most 300 boxes). mAP@50 and mAP@50:95 follow the COCO definition (greedy matching, 101-point interpolation), and our implementation agrees with pycocotools to $10^{-6}$. Precision and recall are pooled over classes at confidence 0.25 and IoU 0.5. Classes without ground truth in a test set are left out of the mean. \emph{Common} classes are those B was trained on (on plant frames: Person, helmet, vest, gloves, boots), and \emph{all} means the nine classes (seven on plant frames). We report mean $\pm$ s.d.\ over seeds (B, P, S) or folds (A), and 95\% confidence intervals from 1{,}000 bootstrap resamples of the 14 recordings. We resample whole recordings because frames of one recording are correlated, and we compute model differences on the same resamples.
