\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

In a controlled comparison without data leakage at an operating phosphoric-acid unit, plant frames raise YOLOv8n from 43.9\% (construction-only benchmark) to 72.5\% mAP@50 on held-out recordings, at 11.7\,ms per frame on a T4 GPU. At equal training-set size, plant frames add 42.3 points (95\% CI [37.8, 47.2]), and more public data alone adds 2.9. When site PPE looks different from public imagery, about 165 annotated site frames help more than 1{,}500 public industrial images. Future work includes plant recordings across days, areas and staff, with absence labels; frame-level video labels to measure false alarms per camera-hour and end-to-end throughput on site hardware; class-aware SH17 sampling and oversampling of rare classes; a 1280-pixel input, or tiled inference on the full $1920\times1080$ frames, for goggles, gloves and gas masks (Fig.~\ref{fig:sizes}); YOLOv8s; and compliance labels to evaluate the band rule.

\section*{Ethics and Data Availability}
The authors recorded the plant footage in the Maroc Chimie phosphoric-acid unit with verbal clearance from OCP representatives for research and educational use. The only people in view are the authors, who consent to the publication of the frames shown. The public datasets are available from the cited sources, the OCP imagery is proprietary and cannot be released, and the code is available at \url{https://github.com/YasserDalali/ppe-cv}.
