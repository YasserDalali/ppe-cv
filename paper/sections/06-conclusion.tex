\section{Conclusion and Future Work}\label{sec:conclusion}

Industrial PPE monitoring fails for lack of sustained attention, not for lack of cameras. We built a detection pipeline that runs on an operating chemical plant's existing IP camera infrastructure: YOLOv8 fine-tuned on a composite, industrially adapted dataset. A detector trained only on the standard construction benchmark reaches 18.5\% mAP@50 on held-out OCP imagery; the adapted model reaches 65.5\%, at 9.35\,ms per frame on commodity hardware. That gap is the paper's core claim: site adaptation, not architectural novelty, is what makes computer-vision-based PPE monitoring work at an operating plant.

Future work follows in priority order. First, a size-matched control to isolate domain coverage from training-set volume. Second, a full-corpus SH17 re-evaluation against a class-aware cap (Section~\ref{sec:methods-dataset}). Third, an ablation to confirm that omitting gas-mask oversampling did not cost recall. Beyond these three, we plan to extend to more OCP sites and seasons, add temporal tracking and re-identification for per-worker compliance, and pursue edge deployment with access-control integration.
