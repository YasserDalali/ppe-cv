\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

In a controlled comparison without data leakage at an operating phosphoric-acid unit, a construction-only YOLOv8n reaches 43.9\% mAP@50 on held-out plant recordings for the classes it knows. The same detector trained with plant frames reaches 72.5\%, at 11.7\,ms per frame on a T4 GPU. At equal training-set size, plant frames add 42.3 points (95\% CI [37.8, 47.2]), and more public data alone adds 2.9. When site PPE looks different from public imagery, about 165 annotated site frames help more than 1{,}500 public industrial images. Small items such as gloves and goggles still need closer views or a higher resolution. Future work includes plant recordings across days, areas and staff, with absence labels; frame-level video labels to measure false alarms per camera-hour and end-to-end throughput on site hardware; class-aware SH17 sampling and oversampling of rare classes; a 1280-pixel input, or tiled inference on the full $1920\times1080$ frames, for goggles, gloves and gas masks, which appear 3 to 7 times smaller at the plant than in public images; and YOLOv8s, compared with a rule that infers absence from person and equipment detections.
