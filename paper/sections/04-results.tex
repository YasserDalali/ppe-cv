\section{Results}\label{sec:results}

All 14 runs completed the full 50 epochs on one T4, taking about 5\,min per B run, 32\,min per P run and 40\,min per S or A run.

\textbf{Plant frames (Table~\ref{tab:main}, Fig.~\ref{fig:bars}).} The construction-only benchmark B reaches 43.9\,$\pm$\,1.0\% mAP@50 on its five classes (seeds: 45.0, 43.7 and 43.1\%). More public data (P$-$B) lowers mAP@50 by 16.6 points, training-set size alone (S$-$P) adds 2.9, and plant frames at equal size (A$-$S) add 42.3 (95\% CI [37.8, 47.2]). No single recording carries the effect. A$-$S is positive in all 1{,}000 resamples and in every fold, by 35.1 to 59.9 points on each fold's own frames. A ranges from 70.0 to 94.0\% across folds. The two folds without gloves score highest (94.0 and 86.0\%) because no model detects gloves (see below). For the same reason A's pooled score (72.5\%) is lower than its per-fold mean (79.9\%), since pooling scores gloves over all 207 frames.

\textbf{Where the gain comes from (Table~\ref{tab:perclass}).} Every model finds persons (95 to 97\%). The gain is in vests, boots and helmets. The plant's ``vest'' is a dark work jacket with reflective bands, and no public model recognizes it as a vest (B 0.5\%, S 0.0\%, A 96.4\%; Fig.~\ref{fig:examples}). Leaving vests out, A$-$S is still 28.8 points and A$-$B 11.7, mostly from boots. Adding public industrial images makes helmets and boots harder to find at the plant: from B to P, helmets drop from 81.3 to 30.1\% and boots from 42.8 to 10.6\%. SH17 supplies 1{,}500 of P's 1{,}970 training images, and its PPE is mostly photographed large and close up, whereas plant PPE is small and seen from a distance. Neither public model detects gas masks, and A reaches 23.4\%. No model detects gloves or goggles (A: 0.0 and 0.1\%). Both are small at plant camera distances, often occluded, and rare (31 and 32 boxes in a few recordings).

\textbf{Public test sets (Table~\ref{tab:other}).} Each model does best on the kind of image it was trained on: B on construction images, P and S on public images, and A on plant frames. Plant frames cost A about one point on the public test set (62.3\,$\pm$\,1.2 vs.\ 63.3\,$\pm$\,0.5\% for S). B, which never saw SH17 or gas-mask images, scores 14.3\% on the common classes of the held-out images and 22.2\% on the public test set.

\textbf{Threshold (Fig.~\ref{fig:pr}a).} On the common classes, the F1-optimal confidence threshold on plant frames is 0.35 for A (F1 85.6\%), 0.28 for B (54.1\%), 0.43 for P and 0.33 for S. The 0.25 we use for P/R is below every model's optimum and closest to B's, so it does not favor A.

\textbf{Speed.} We measured speed offline on a Tesla T4 (Google Colab) at batch size 1, over 200 plant frames ($1920\times1080$) after 10 warm-up frames. One A model needs 11.7\,ms per frame (2.0\,ms pre-processing, 8.5\,ms inference, 1.2\,ms NMS). The figure covers the model only. It excludes video decoding, network transfer and alert handling, and we did not measure the end-to-end throughput of a multi-camera deployment.

\textbf{Error analysis (Fig.~\ref{fig:pr}b).} Most of A's errors are missed objects. Of 1{,}007 plant objects, 76.4\% are found, 21.6\% are missed and 2.0\% get the wrong class, mainly boots read as helmets (8 cases). A finds 95.7\% of vests, 81.7\% of helmets and 76.2\% of boots, but only 18.6\% of gas masks and no gloves or goggles. These three are the smallest objects (Fig.~\ref{fig:sizes}). In the 640-pixel model input, the median plant goggle is 11 pixels across (57 in public training images), a gas mask 23 (170, mostly face close-ups) and a glove 12 (34). Plant helmets and boots are also small (23 and 18 pixels), but A has many plant examples of them. A sees most gas masks but with low confidence: 41 of 59 have a matching box at some confidence, and 11 above 0.25. Its goggle boxes are poorly placed, and gloves are not detected at all. Our compute budget (14 runs on one T4 GPU) kept the input at 640 pixels. A 1280-pixel input would double the size of these objects, at about four times the computation. Over the 207 frames, 153 detections match no object, most of them boots (60). Plant frames have no absence labels, so we cannot assess the absence classes here.
