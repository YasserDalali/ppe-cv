The generic detector is respectable on its own imagery (59.1\% mAP@50) but collapses on industrial imagery: 0.4\% industrial proxy, 18.5\% OCP, against 49.6\% and 65.5\% adapted. Two points follow. First, the industrial-proxy collapse is more informative: that set is still generic industrial imagery, not OCP-specific, so its near-total collapse shows ordinary domain shift alone defeats the generic model, before dust, angles, or uniforms enter the picture. Second, the OCP gap (18.5\% vs.\ 65.5\%) is the deployment-relevant number the abstract and conclusion lead with. Part of the collapse is plausibly a calibration artifact (fixed conf=0.25, tuned implicitly on construction imagery). This is one reason Section~\ref{sec:discussion} treats the training-set-size confound as open, not settled.

\subsection{Training Performance}\label{sec:results-training}

Over the 50-epoch run, box, classification, and DFL losses decline from 1.38, 2.55, 1.39 to 0.90, 0.59, 1.04, validation tracking training with no overfitting divergence. The validation curve stabilizes by epoch 25: mAP@50 closes at 68.4\% (mAP@50--95 40.6\%); recall plateaus near 60--66\%; precision, noisier, ends at 74.4\%. Headline values elsewhere come from a separate \texttt{best.pt} evaluation on val, so Table~\ref{tab:perclass}'s all-classes row (78.0/63.5/68.6\%) differs slightly from the epoch-50 curve: it scores all ten classes, versus the seven shared with the generic baseline (Section~\ref{sec:methods-comparison}) used elsewhere, answering different questions.

\subsection{Error Analysis}\label{sec:results-errors}

The confusion matrix (Fig.~\ref{fig:confusion}) shows missed detection, not equipped/unequipped confusion, dominates: the largest off-diagonal mass is always the background row. Helmet: 59\% correct, 37\% missed, 6\% confused with no\_helmet; similarly no\_gloves (47\% correct) and boots (70\% correct, 24\% missed). gloves is the weakest diagonal (33\% correct, matching 33.8\% mAP@50), thin, occluded, hard at IP camera range (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}). no\_goggle has no row/column at all (zero training/validation instances), so its 0.0\% row is data absence, not failure (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}); the same thinness affects no\_helmet (12) and no\_gloves (33). A class-aware SH17 cap (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}) could separate genuine weakness from support scarcity in future runs.
